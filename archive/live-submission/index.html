Forecast details: how “getting off” is estimated

Baseline: the share of riders who got off at each door for the same weekday and 15-minute slot, averaged over the latest 2 weeks of history. The history is 4 weeks of sample data standing in for MTR records. When an exit line counts real people getting off, that train is added and today’s correction updates. Counts from the virtual CCTV are kept for this session only. Nothing else is learned or trained.
