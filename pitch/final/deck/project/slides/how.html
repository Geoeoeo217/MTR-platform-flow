How it works

From camera to lights in five steps

1

Count

Camera AI counts the queue at every door

2

Forecast

How many get off each car, from history

3

Capacity

Space at each door on the next train

4

Recommend

Best door, with the reason shown

5

Guide

Lights, sign and a GenAI announcement

space = car capacity − people on board + people getting off

queue ÷ space → green < 0.7 · amber < 1.0 · red

1:15–1:45 (30 s). Five steps, each using the last one’s output. A camera AI counts the queue at each door. A forecast estimates how many people will get off each car from history. That gives the space at each door on the next train. The system recommends the best door and shows why, then sets the lights and writes the announcement. Every decision is explainable with this one formula.
