The problem

Rush hour packs a few doors while others stay empty

People queue by their tunnel. Passengers stop at the doors nearest to where they walk onto the platform.

They can’t see ahead. At stations like Admiralty, the platform and the train are hidden until you leave the corridor.

Space goes unused. Some doors overflow and people miss the train, while doors further along still have room.

Our simulator: without guidance, doors next to the tunnels overflow (red).
