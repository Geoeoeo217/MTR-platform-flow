Green: go here. Room predicted on the next train.

Amber: limited. Some room, filling up.

Lights sit in the access tunnels and above every platform door, and update every 2 seconds.

0:45–1:15 (30 s). Our solution is simple for passengers: green, amber and red lights. Green means the AI predicts room at that door on the next train; amber means limited; red means full or about to be. The key is where they go: in the access tunnels, so people choose before they reach the platform, and above every door, so they get updated guidance as things change.
