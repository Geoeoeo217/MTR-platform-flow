How it works

From data to lights in five steps

1

Collect

Queues per door, load per car, historical alighting

2

Predict

How many get off each car, so the room at each door

3

Recommend

Compare people heading to a door with its room

4

Light up

Tunnel signs and door lights, every 2 seconds

5

Evaluate

Score evenness and feed real alighting back

room = capacity − load on arrival + forecast getting off

ratio = people queuing or walking there ÷ room → green < 0.7 · amber < 1.0 · red

1:15–1:55 (40 s). The system runs a five-step loop. It collects three kinds of data: how many people are queuing at each door from CCTV, how full each car of the approaching train is from the train’s own load sensors, and historical data on how many people get off at each door by station, day and hour. It predicts the room each door will have, compares that with the people already queuing or walking there, and sets the lights. Every train, it checks how many really got off and feeds that back in.
