The role of AI

Three jobs the AI does on every train

Forecast

Predicts how many people get off at each door. Starts from history, then learns from every train.

Error 5.6 → 3.8 people per car (−32%)

Decide

Sets each light from predicted room, not just today’s queue. A busy door stays green if many will get off there.

Prevent herding

Spreads people across all green doors by spare room, so one green door never becomes the next crowd.

1:55–2:25 (30 s). The AI does three real jobs. First, it forecasts how many people will get off at each door. It starts from the historical average and corrects itself after every train; in our tests its error fell by a third. Second, it decides the lights from predicted room, not just the current queue. Third, it prevents herding: if everyone ran to the one green door, that door would become the new problem, so the system spreads people across all the green doors.
